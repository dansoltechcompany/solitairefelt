One killer grid per day. Cage layout seeded by the calendar. It sits in our Sudoku & Numbers collection because people already search for this mechanic by name. The rules below match the familiar version of the game, with original board art and a full felt table for play.

How to play Daily Killer Sudoku

Fill 1–9 so each row, column, and 3×3 box is unique. Dotted cages show a sum; digits in a cage cannot repeat and must add to that clue. No given digits — the cage sums are the puzzle.

Tips

Start with cages of only two cells — they pin a unique pair. Box lines still work; cages just add extra elimination.
