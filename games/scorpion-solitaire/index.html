Build down by suit and scoop any face-up card. It sits in our Solitaire & Cards collection because people already search for this mechanic by name. The rules below match the familiar version of the game, with original board art and a full felt table for play.

How to play Scorpion Solitaire

Build down by suit. You may move any face-up card together with the cards covering it. Three leftover cards deal onto the first three columns.

Tips

Expose face-down cards before emptying a column for style points. Do not always empty a king slot if it hides useful aces.
