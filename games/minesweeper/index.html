How to play Minesweeper Beginner

Click to open a cell. Numbers count adjacent mines. Flag suspects. The first click is always safe. Intermediate is 16×16 with 40 mines; expert is 30×16 with 99.

Tips

Chord on a satisfied number by clicking it when its flags are placed — here, open neighbors with a quick second click on the number.
